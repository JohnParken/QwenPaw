# Writing

Use this skill for drafting, rewriting, summarising, and structuring
professional text. Keep claims grounded in the supplied context, preserve
the requested tone, and leave unresolved facts clearly marked for the user.

Text files are UTF-8. Document, spreadsheet, presentation, and PDF output is
delegated to the corresponding specialised skill.

The controlled `scripts/normalize_text.py` entrypoint may be used for bounded
whitespace normalization.
